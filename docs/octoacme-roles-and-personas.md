# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define quality strategies, own testing approaches, and validate acceptance criteria. They partner with developers and product teams to ensure features meet quality standards and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each project
- Create test plans and acceptance criteria validation checklists
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Collaborate on Definition of Done to include quality gates
- Triage defects and work with developers on fixes

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, production-ready features
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Continuously improve testing efficiency and coverage

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Test plan reviews with product and engineering
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Post-release quality verification reports

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on testability, review test coverage, discuss quality approaches
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria, align on quality bar, discuss trade-offs between speed and quality
- **With Project Managers**: Provide quality metrics, flag quality blockers, coordinate testing schedules

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, mentor developers, and manage technical risks. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Drive technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and debt
- Mentor developers and review technical solutions
- Ensure compliance with security, performance, and scalability standards
- Advocate for refactoring and technical improvements

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and future rework
- Build scalable systems that support growth

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings
- Technical design document reviews
- Code reviews and technical mentoring
- Technical risk register and mitigation planning

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Guide design decisions, review technical approaches, mentor on best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility, estimate effort for different approaches, advise on scalability
- **With Project Managers**: Flag technical risks, provide effort estimates, identify dependencies

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business and technical teams by gathering requirements, clarifying business needs, and ensuring solutions align with intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Translate business needs into technical specifications
- Clarify ambiguous requirements with stakeholders
- Validate solutions against business goals
- Create user stories and acceptance criteria in collaboration with product and engineering
- Document business processes and data flows

### Goals
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements
- Ensure technical solutions address real business problems
- Improve communication between business and engineering teams

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
- User story and acceptance criteria workshops
- Requirement review meetings
- Solution validation sessions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify product vision, translate into actionable requirements, validate solutions
- **With Developers**: Explain business context, answer requirement questions, validate implementations
- **With Project Managers**: Provide requirement status, flag scope ambiguities, support timeline planning

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approvals for projects. They represent organizational priorities and ensure alignment with broader business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide strategic direction and priority guidance
- Approve project charter and major decisions
- Communicate project importance across the organization
- Monitor project progress against business goals
- Escalate blockers and remove organizational barriers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Maximize return on investment and resource utilization

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and charter review meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and dashboard reviews
- Decision gates and approval points
- Executive briefings and board-level reporting

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Define business priorities, provide strategic context, approve roadmap
- **With Project Managers**: Review timelines, approve scope changes, escalate organizational blockers
- **With Developers and QA**: Provide business rationale for features, understand technical constraints
